The ideas on this site are adapted, in plain language and for physicians, from the sources below. Go to them if you want more depth.

## Context engineering

- [Effective context engineering for AI agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents), Anthropic Engineering. The origin of "context rot," the "smallest set of high-signal" information principle, just-in-time retrieval, and structured note-taking. Technical, but the first half is readable for anyone.

## The Four C's

- [How to Build an AI Second Brain with the Four C's Framework](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/ai-second-brain-four-cs-framework-context-connections-capabilities-cadence), MindStudio. The Context, Connections, Capabilities, Cadence framework this site is built around.
- [The Four C's of an AI Operating System](https://jordanborden.com/resources/four-cs-ai-operating-system), Jordan Borden.

## Knowledge bases and the "LLM wiki"

- [Andrej Karpathy on LLM knowledge bases](https://deepakness.com/raw/llm-knowledge-bases/): keeping raw sources in one folder and letting the AI compile and maintain a linked wiki, with answers filed back in.
- [karpathy-llm-wiki](https://github.com/julianoczkowski/karpathy-llm-wiki): a step-by-step guide to the same method with Claude and Obsidian.

## Organizing your notes

- [The PARA Method](https://thomasjfrank.com/productivity/books/the-para-method-by-tiago-forte-summary-and-book-notes/), summary of Tiago Forte's book on Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives.
- [Obsidian help](https://help.obsidian.md): the official documentation.
