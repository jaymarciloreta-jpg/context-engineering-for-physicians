The whole method on one page. Print it (Ctrl or Cmd + P) and keep it by your desk.

## The Four C's

| | Question | Your move |
|---|---|---|
| **Context** | What does the AI know about me? | `CLAUDE.md` at the top of the vault, a brief in every project |
| **Connections** | What can it reach? | One connector at a time, calendar first, nothing with patient data |
| **Capabilities** | What can it do? | Recipes in `skills/` for anything you do three times |
| **Cadence** | When does it run? | One scheduled task that writes you a note |

## Your folders

`00 Inbox` new · `01 Projects` work with an end · `02 Areas` ongoing roles · `03 Resources` reference · `04 Archive` done · `Daily` · `Templates` · `skills`

## Prompts you will use every day

| When | Say |
|---|---|
| Starting a project session | "Let's work on [project]. Read its brief and tell me where we are." |
| Ending a session | "Update Status and Next in this project's CLAUDE.md and add a line to log.md." |
| Running a recipe | "Run the recipe in skills/[name].md." |
| Saving a new recipe | "Save the steps we just used as a recipe in skills/[name].md." |
| It repeats a mistake | "Add a rule to CLAUDE.md so this does not happen again." |
| Checking its work | "For each claim, give the source and quote the exact sentence." |
| Getting a second opinion | "Review this as a skeptical reviewer. What is wrong or unsupported?" |
| Filing notes | "Sort 00 Inbox into the right folders. Show me the plan before moving anything." |
| Weekly overview | "Read every project brief and give me a table: status, next step, deadline, stuck?" |
| Scheduling | "Run the recipe in skills/[name].md every weekday at 6 am." |

## Five rules

1. **No identifiable patient information** in anything not approved for it.
2. **Draft, review, approve.** The AI drafts; you send.
3. **Move, never delete.** Finished work goes to `04 Archive`.
4. **One task per conversation.** Start fresh when you switch topics.
5. **Verify anything clinical or numeric** against the source.

## When something goes wrong

Generic output → add detail to `CLAUDE.md`. Forgot a rule → put it in a file, not the chat. Made-up citation → ask for the quote, open the source. Scheduled task empty → check the connector sign-in and folder names.
