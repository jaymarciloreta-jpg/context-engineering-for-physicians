The spine of this wiki. Read them in order; each builds on the one before.

1. [[C1 - Context]]: what the AI knows about you. Plain notes, a CLAUDE.md file, a tidy folder layout.
2. [[C2 - Connections]]: what the AI can reach. Connectors to email, calendar, files, and references, with firm lines around patient data.
3. [[C3 - Capabilities]]: what the AI can do. Saved recipes (skills) for the tasks you repeat.
4. [[C4 - Cadence]]: when the AI runs. Scheduled tasks that work while you are in clinic or the OR.

The framework is adapted, in plain language and for physicians, from the sources listed in [[Sources and Further Reading]].
