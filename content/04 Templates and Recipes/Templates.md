Copy these into your vault and fill in the brackets. Want a head start? See [[Example CLAUDE Files|example CLAUDE.md files for eight specialties and roles]]. Shorter is better: a page you keep current beats three pages you do not.

## Your CLAUDE.md (top of the vault)

```markdown
# About me

[Name], [specialty / subspecialty], [practice setting: academic, private, VA...].
Roles: [clinical], [research], [teaching], [admin/leadership].
Typical week: [e.g. clinic Mon/Wed, OR Tue/Thu, research Fri].

## How I write
- Patients: [reading level, tone, format].
- Colleagues: [e.g. concise, lead with the point].
- Trainees: [e.g. explain the why].
- Administrators: [e.g. brief, bottom line first].
- Sign-off: [your standard sign-off].

## What I want help with most
- [e.g. email triage, weekly planning, manuscripts, talks].

## How this vault is organized
- 00 Inbox: unsorted new notes. File them into the folders below.
- 01 Projects: active work with an end date. Each has its own CLAUDE.md brief.
- 02 Areas: ongoing responsibilities (clinic, research program, teaching).
- 03 Resources: reference material and templates.
- 04 Archive: finished work. Move here; never delete.
- Daily: one note per day. skills: my saved recipes.

## Hard rules
- Never include identifiable patient information in anything.
- Never send email, post, or share anything without my explicit approval.
- Never delete files. Move them to 04 Archive.
- Ask me when unsure rather than guess. Cite sources for clinical claims,
  and quote the exact sentence you relied on.
- [Any others: e.g. US spelling, AMA citation style.]
```

## Project brief (CLAUDE.md inside a project folder)

```markdown
# Project: [name]

Goal: [one sentence, with the finish line].
Deadline: [date].
Team: [names and roles].
Status: [where things stand, one or two lines].
Next: [the next three concrete steps].

## Key files
- [drafts/main-draft.md]: [what it is].

## Rules for this project
- [e.g. journal style, word limits, do not change methods without asking].
```

## Area note (in 02 Areas)

```markdown
---
status: active
tags: [area, teaching]
---
What this is: [e.g. residency teaching responsibilities].
People: [who is involved].
Recurring work: [e.g. monthly lecture, quarterly evaluations].
Standards: [what good looks like].
Open items: [anything pending].
```
