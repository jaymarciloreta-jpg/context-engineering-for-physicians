A ready-made Obsidian vault so you can skip the setup and start filling in.

**[Download the Physician Starter Vault (zip)](https://jaymarciloreta-jpg.github.io/context-engineering-for-physicians/files/physician-starter-vault.zip)**

You can also [browse every file on GitHub](https://github.com/jaymarciloreta-jpg/context-engineering-for-physicians/tree/main/starter-vault) before downloading.

## What is inside

```text
Physician Starter Vault/
  README.md              how to use the kit
  CLAUDE.md              your about-me file, with brackets to fill in
  00 Inbox/              new notes land here
  01 Projects/
    Example Project - Grand Rounds Talk/
      CLAUDE.md          a sample project brief
      log.md
  02 Areas/              Clinic, Research Program, Teaching, Committees
  03 Resources/          Teaching, Guidelines
  04 Archive/
  Daily/
  Templates/             Project Brief, Area Note, Daily Note
  skills/                13 ready-made recipes
```

The 13 recipes: patient handout, thank-you letter, abstract, paper summary, email triage, meeting actions, weekly plan, talk outline, vault tidy-up, morning brief, evening sort, weekly digest, and literature watch. Each is explained on the [[Recipe Library]] and [[Scheduled Recipes]] pages.

## Set it up in four steps

1. Unzip the download somewhere you can find it, for example in `Documents`.
2. In Obsidian, choose **Open folder as vault** and pick the unzipped folder.
3. Open `CLAUDE.md` and fill in the brackets. Keep it under one page. See [[C1 - Context]] for guidance.
4. In the Claude desktop app, start a task on the folder and say: "Read CLAUDE.md and the README, then help me set up my first real project."

Or run the [[Interactive Tutorial]] on this folder and Claude will walk you through it.

## Already have a vault?

Do not overwrite it. Copy just the pieces you want (usually `CLAUDE.md`, `Templates`, and `skills`) into your existing vault, then ask Claude to propose how your current notes map onto the PARA folders before moving anything.
