About an hour. Block it on your calendar. At the end you will have a folder layout, an "about me" file, your first project, and your first recipe. Everything after this is just adding to it.

> [!tip] Shortcut
> Download the [[Starter Kit]], unzip it, and open it as a vault in Obsidian. It already contains everything below, with blanks for you to fill in. Or let Claude do it with you using the [[Interactive Tutorial]].

## Step 1: Create the folders (5 minutes)

In your vault, create these folders. The numbers keep them in order.

```text
00 Inbox/        new notes land here until filed
01 Projects/     active work with an end date (a paper, a grant, a talk)
02 Areas/        ongoing responsibilities (clinic, research program, teaching)
03 Resources/    reference material (guidelines, templates, how-tos)
04 Archive/      finished or inactive items
Daily/           one note per day
Templates/       note templates
skills/          your saved recipes
```

This is the **PARA** layout (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archive). Why it works is explained in [[C1 - Context]].

Or just ask Claude:

```text
Create the PARA folder layout in this vault: 00 Inbox, 01 Projects, 02 Areas,
03 Resources, 04 Archive, Daily, Templates, and skills.
```

## Step 2: Write your about-me file (15 minutes)

Create `CLAUDE.md` at the top of the vault. Use the template on the [[Templates]] page. Fill in:

- Who you are: specialty, practice setting, roles (clinical, research, teaching, admin).
- How you write, for each audience: patients, colleagues, trainees, administrators.
- What you want help with most.
- Hard rules: what the AI must never do.
- How the vault is organized (copy the folder list above).

Keep it under one page. Short and specific beats long and vague.

## Step 3: Set up your first project (15 minutes)

Pick one real, active piece of work, such as a paper, a talk, or a committee task. Create a folder for it in `01 Projects`, and inside it a short brief called `CLAUDE.md` (Claude reads these too when it works in that folder). Use the project template on the [[Templates]] page:

- Goal and deadline.
- Who is involved.
- Where things stand.
- The next three steps.

Drop the related files (drafts, PDFs, notes) into that folder.

## Step 4: Fill your areas (10 minutes)

In `02 Areas`, create one note per ongoing responsibility: `Clinic`, `Research Program`, `Teaching`, `Committees`. One paragraph each is enough for now: what it is, who is involved, what recurring work it creates.

## Step 5: Add your first recipe (10 minutes)

Copy one recipe from the [[Recipe Library]] into `skills/`. The weekly plan or email triage recipe is a good first choice. Run it once:

```text
Run the recipe in skills/weekly-plan.md.
```

## Step 6: Test the whole thing (5 minutes)

Start a fresh task in Claude on the vault and ask:

```text
Based on what you can see in this vault, what do you know about me,
what am I working on, and what should I do next on my project?
```

If the answer is specific and accurate, your setup works. If it is vague, your `CLAUDE.md` or project brief needs more detail. Fix it and ask again.

## What comes next

Live with it for a week. Each time you notice yourself re-explaining something, add it to a context file. Then add one [[C2 - Connections|connection]], then one [[C4 - Cadence|scheduled task]].
