Most clinicians have a list of "someone should build this" ideas from the OR or clinic. AI is a remarkably good thinking partner for taking one of those from a napkin sketch to something you can show an engineer or a tech transfer office.

## An idea folder

Give each serious idea its own project folder with a brief:

```markdown
# Idea: [short name]

## The clinical problem
What goes wrong today, how often, for whom. Be concrete.

## Current workarounds
What surgeons do now and why it falls short.

## My idea
One paragraph. Sketches go in this folder.

## Constraints
Sterilization, single-use or reusable, size limits, what it must attach to.

## Status and next step
```

Writing the clinical problem clearly is the most valuable part, and it is the part only you can do.

## Recipes for ideas

- **Pressure-test it:** "Read the brief. Act as a skeptical medical device engineer and a skeptical hospital purchaser. List the ten hardest questions each would ask."
- **Prior art scan:** "Search for existing products and published approaches that solve this problem. Summarize each and how my idea differs." Treat this as a starting point; it is not a patent search.
- **Need statement:** "Write a need statement in the Stanford Biodesign format: a way to [address problem] in [population] that [outcome]."
- **Design options:** "Propose three different design approaches, with tradeoffs in a table."

## Toward CAD

AI can help you get from words to early 3D shapes:

- Describe the part in plain language with rough dimensions, and ask for a parametric model script (for example in OpenSCAD) or step-by-step instructions for a CAD tool like Fusion or Onshape.
- Some CAD and 3D tools now have AI connectors, so the AI can build or modify a model directly.
- Use the result to communicate the idea, not as a final design. Real engineering, testing, and regulatory work come later with the right partners.

> [!warning] Protect your idea
> Before sharing a potentially patentable idea in any tool, check your institution's policies on invention disclosure and which AI tools are approved for confidential work. Talk to your tech transfer office early.
