**Goal:** a one-page about-me file that Claude reads before every task. **Time:** 15 minutes.

This is the single most valuable file in your setup. Every answer you get from now on is shaped by it.

## What goes in it

1. **About me:** specialty, setting, roles, typical week.
2. **How I write:** one line each for patients, colleagues, trainees, administrators. Your sign-off.
3. **What I want help with most:** your top three or four time sinks.
4. **How this vault is organized:** the folder list from lesson 3.
5. **Hard rules:** at minimum, these four:
    - Never include identifiable patient information.
    - Never send, post, or share anything without my approval.
    - Never delete files; move them to 04 Archive.
    - Ask when unsure. Cite sources for clinical claims.

The fill-in-the-blanks version is on [[Templates]].

## Do it with Claude

Let Claude interview you instead of writing it yourself:

```text
Interview me to write my CLAUDE.md. Ask me two or three questions at a time
about my roles, my week, how I write for each audience, what I want help
with, and my hard rules. Then draft it using the sections About me, How I
write, What I want help with most, How this vault is organized, and Hard
rules. Keep it under one page. Show me before saving.
```

## Checkpoint

- [ ] `CLAUDE.md` at the top of your vault, under one page.
- [ ] It has all five sections, including the four hard rules.

**The habit that makes it compound:** whenever you catch yourself re-explaining something, add it here. "Add a rule to CLAUDE.md so handouts stay under one page."
