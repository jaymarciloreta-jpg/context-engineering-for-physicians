**Goal:** a folder layout that tells the AI what is active and where new things go. **Time:** 10 minutes.

## The idea in one minute

**PARA** sorts everything into four places:

- **Projects:** work with an end (a paper, a talk, a grant).
- **Areas:** ongoing roles with no end (clinic, research program, teaching).
- **Resources:** reference material (guidelines, templates).
- **Archive:** finished work. Move things here; never delete.

Plus three helpers: an **Inbox** for new notes, **Daily** for one note per day, and **skills** for your recipes.

## Steps

Create these folders (or skip this if you used the [[Starter Kit]]):

```text
00 Inbox/   01 Projects/   02 Areas/   03 Resources/   04 Archive/
Daily/      Templates/     skills/
```

In Obsidian settings, set **Default location for new notes** to `00 Inbox` and the Templates folder to `Templates`.

## Do it with Claude

```text
Create the PARA folders in this vault: 00 Inbox, 01 Projects, 02 Areas,
03 Resources, 04 Archive, Daily, Templates, skills. If I already have notes,
show me a table of where each one would go. Do not move anything until I approve.
```

## Checkpoint

- [ ] The eight folders exist.
- [ ] Any older notes are filed or still waiting in `00 Inbox`.
