**Goal:** one real project that Claude can pick up exactly where you left off. **Time:** 15 minutes.

## Steps

1. **Pick one active project:** a paper, a talk, a grant, a QI initiative, a committee task.
2. **Make its folder** in `01 Projects`, named clearly (`Olfaction Outcomes Paper`, not `Project 3`).
3. **Add a short brief** named `CLAUDE.md` inside that folder:

    ```markdown
    # Project: [name]
    Goal: [one sentence, with the finish line]
    Deadline: [date]
    Team: [names and roles]
    Status: [where things stand]
    Next: [three concrete steps]
    Rules: [anything specific, e.g. journal style]
    ```

4. **Drop in the related files:** drafts, PDFs, notes.
5. **Do one small piece of real work** on it with Claude.

## Do it with Claude

Start of every session:

```text
Let's work on [project]. Read its brief and tell me where we are and what
the next step is.
```

End of every session (this ten-second step is what makes the system work):

```text
Update the Status and Next lines in this project's CLAUDE.md and add one
line to log.md about what we did today.
```

## Checkpoint

- [ ] A project folder with a `CLAUDE.md` brief.
- [ ] You did one piece of real work and updated the brief afterward.
