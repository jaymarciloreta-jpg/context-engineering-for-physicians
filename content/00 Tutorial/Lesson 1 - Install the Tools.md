**Goal:** Obsidian and Claude installed and pointed at the same folder. **Time:** 20 minutes.

## Steps

1. **Install Obsidian** from [obsidian.md](https://obsidian.md). Choose **Create new vault**, name it `Second Brain`, and put it in `Documents`. (Or unzip the [[Starter Kit]] and choose **Open folder as vault**.)
2. **Turn on two core plugins** in Obsidian settings: Daily notes and Templates.
3. **Get a paid Claude plan** (Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise). Ask first whether your institution offers an approved one.
4. **Install the Claude desktop app** from [claude.ai/download](https://claude.ai/download) and sign in.
5. **Point Claude at your vault.** Start a new task in the desktop app and choose your `Second Brain` folder as the place Claude works.

## Do it with Claude

Once step 5 is done, paste this to confirm everything works:

```text
List what is in this folder. Then explain, in three sentences, what you can
and cannot do with the files here.
```

## Checkpoint

- [ ] Obsidian opens your vault.
- [ ] Claude can list the folder's contents.
