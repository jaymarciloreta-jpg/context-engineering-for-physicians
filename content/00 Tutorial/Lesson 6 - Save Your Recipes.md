**Goal:** two saved recipes for tasks you repeat every week. **Time:** 10 minutes.

A recipe (Claude calls it a **skill**) is a plain-text checklist the AI follows. You already made one in lesson 2. Now add two more for your biggest weekly time sinks.

## Steps

1. **Pick two** from the [[Recipe Library]]. Good first choices:
    - **Weekly plan:** turns your calendar and project briefs into a plan.
    - **Meeting note to action items:** files the note and pulls out to-dos.
    - **Email triage:** sorts the inbox and drafts replies (needs lesson 7).
    - **Paper summary:** one page per paper, against your question.
    - **Talk outline:** a slide-by-slide plan from your rough notes.
2. **Copy each into** `skills/` as its own file.
3. **Run one** to see it work.

## Do it with Claude

```text
Read CLAUDE.md. Write me two recipes in skills/: one for [task] and one for
[task]. Follow the safe pattern: read my context, do the work, save a draft,
show me, never send or delete anything. Then run the first one.
```

Run any recipe later with: "Run the recipe in skills/weekly-plan.md."

## Checkpoint

- [ ] At least three recipes in `skills/`.
- [ ] You ran one and the result was useful.
